##  Assignment 

The management has an enhancement request to the executive summary dashboard-style page. They want you to add a breakdown of profit margin by product. You will create a new treemap to add to the `Executive Summary` page and adjust the interactions between the new visual and the existing visuals.

- Click on the **Filter** image above the **Stacked bar chart** visual showing the total sales by product description and category.
